A useful starting point

Give your next practice session a purpose.

Use a simple plan to make your notes more useful than “hit a bucket.”

01 · Pick one skill

Choose a narrow focus such as start direction or distance control. Use the facility’s rules and safe practice areas. A short session with a clear focus is easier to review than a list of unrelated experiments.

02 · Keep the conditions visible

Note the club, target and practice setup. Choose a repeatable task and record what happened without treating one session as proof of a permanent change.

03 · Finish with one observation

Write what felt repeatable, what remained inconsistent and what to try next. If you work with a coach, bring the notes so the next lesson can start with specific examples.
