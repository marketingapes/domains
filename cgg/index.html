The crazy manifesto

Crazy is a compliment here.

Golf has a reputation: quiet, collared, slightly afraid of fun. The game never agreed. Golfers have been inventing bets, partner swaps and ridiculous penalties for as long as two of them have shared a tee. That's the golf we're here for, whether you're scratch or still counting on your fingers.

Side games for every kind of chaos

Real formats with real rules. Each one has a full page, a worked example and a scorecard that does the adding up.

All side games
4 playersWolf

Pick a partner after their drive, or go Lone Wolf and bet on yourself. Ego is part of the format.

2 to 6Snake

Three-putt and you hold the snake. Hold it on 18 and you pay. Suddenly every two-footer has a pulse.

2 to 6Skins

Win a hole outright, take the skin. Tie, and it rolls over. Four ties later, a tap-in is worth a fortune.

2 v 2Vegas

Your team's two scores glued into one number. A 4 and a 5 is 45. An 8 is a disaster you can see from space.

3 to 6Bingo Bango Bongo

First on the green, closest to the pin, first in the hole. The 24-handicap can win this. That's the point.

2 or 2 v 2Nassau

Front nine, back nine, overall: three bets in one. A terrible front nine is just a plot twist.

Playing for money? Keep it friendly and legal where you live. The best stake is one nobody minds losing.

Game of the week

Wolf: pick a partner. Or go rogue.

Wolf turns every tee box into a decision. One player is the Wolf on each hole, watches the drives, and chooses a partner or goes it alone. It's the best four-player game for keeping everyone involved on every hole.

  1. The Wolf rotates every hole.
  2. After each drive, the Wolf picks that player as a partner, or waits for the next one and hopes.
  3. Or goes Lone Wolf for bigger points. Win and you're a legend; lose and everyone else gets paid.

The Putting Ladder

Time 15 minutesYou need 5 balls, 4 tees

Most three-putts start with a first putt that finishes too far away, not with a missed short one. The ladder drill trains the speed that gets your first putt close, and its scoring game makes 15 minutes on the practice green go fast.
