Game of the week

Wolf: pick a partner, or go it alone

Wolf turns every tee box into a decision. One player is the Wolf on each hole, watches the drives, and chooses a partner or goes it alone. It's the best four-player game for keeping everyone involved on every hole.

  1. Rotate who's the Wolf every hole.
  2. The Wolf picks a partner straight after that player's drive, or passes.
  3. Go Lone Wolf for bigger points. Win and you're a hero; lose and everyone else scores.

Drill of the week · Putting

The Putting Ladder

Most three-putts start with a first putt that finishes too far away, not with a missed short one. The ladder drill trains the speed that gets your first putt close, and its scoring game makes 15 minutes on the practice green go fast.

Time: 15 minutes · You need: 5 balls, 4 tees

Gear pick

A putting mat you'll actually use

If you only buy one piece of practice gear this winter, make it a putting mat long enough for the ladder drill. Check the length against your hallway, then decide whether you want a ball return.
