Before you buy a big golf gift

  • Measure the room. Launch monitors and enclosures list minimum ceiling height, width and depth. Check with a full swing in mind.
  • Read what's included. Some launch monitors need a subscription for full features.
  • Check the return policy and shipping time. Big parcels and personalised balls take longer, and returns on large items can cost money.
  • Buy for the golfer, not the discount. If they won't use it every week, a smaller gift wins.

The best free gift in golf

A standing Saturday game. Print a Wolf scorecard, a Nassau tracker or a Skins calculator, put it in a card, and invite them. More gift ideas by budget are in our Crazy Golf Gift Guide 2026.
