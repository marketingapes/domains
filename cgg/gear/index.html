Picks by category

Worth it if you'll use it

Gift Guide 2026

Putting mat or home green

The most-used piece of practice gear for most golfers. Check length (longer allows lag practice), surface speed and whether you want a ball return. Measure your space first.

Good for: Anyone who wants to stop three-putting

Compare putting mats

Launch monitor or home simulator

A big purchase that only makes sense with the space and the habit to use it. Check ceiling height and room depth against the maker's requirements, and whether full features need a subscription.

Good for: Regular golfers with a garage or spare room

Browse simulators and launch monitors

Golf balls

For casual golf, bright or matte coloured balls are easier to find. For your main game, pick one model and stick with it so every short-game shot reacts the same way.

Good for: Everyone; personalised sleeves make good gifts

See Vice Golf balls

Pre-owned clubs

GOLF Partner is a used-club shop that ships pre-owned clubs from Japan and grades each one by condition. It is a way to try a different putter head shape without paying new-club money.

Good for: Trying a new putter or a starter set

Browse pre-owned putters See club sets

Backyard putting greens

A putting and chipping green turns part of a lawn or patio into a practice hole. The Indoor Golf Shop carries the Big Moss range, including an outdoor putting target green.

Good for: Families and party hosts

See Big Moss greens

What you can skip

  • Gadgets that promise a new swing. A couple of alignment sticks do most of what they do.
  • Premium balls for practice and mini golf. A basic ball rolls fine on a short putt.
  • A full new set before you know you love the game. Rent, borrow or start with a half set.
  • Anything you would only use once a year.

How we choose stores
