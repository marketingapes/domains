Business workflow review

Start with the searches that matter.

A scoped review of search results and the website changes within your control.

01 · Capture a useful baseline

Record the exact query, date, location context and relevant URLs. Results can vary between searches, so a single screenshot is a starting observation rather than a universal ranking.

02 · Identify who controls each change

Separate pages you own from third-party listings and websites. Updates to your own content are different from requests to another publisher. Record the owner and next action for each item.

03 · Agree a bounded work list

Prioritize specific deliverables and a review date. Rankings, indexing and third-party removals cannot be guaranteed. A proposed paid scope should describe the work, not promise a search-engine outcome.

Bring these four things

Request a search-results review

Email a short description of your business process. Do not include passwords, customer records or confidential information. Scope, price and timing are agreed separately.
