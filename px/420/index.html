How the 420 Exchange works

It runs on the same swap system as the rest of Pillow Exchange: pillow for pillow, no money changing hands. Just greener.

Post your pillow

Snap a few photos of the leaf-print, tie-dye or smiley pillow you are done with. Add the size, condition and what you would love in return. About two minutes, tops.

Roll with the offers

Other members who dig your pillow offer one of theirs. You see their photos and details and decide if the vibes match.

Swap and chill

Accept an offer and you can both see each other's email in My swaps. Ship it or meet somewhere public, then go put your feet up.

Pro tip: put 420 in your pillow's title or description so other 420 swappers can find it with search. See what's up for swap.

We swap pillows, not plants.

The 420 Exchange is a joke-loving corner of a throw pillow swap site. Here is the not-joking part:

  • No actual weed, seeds, edibles, oils, vapes or paraphernalia. Not in a listing, not in a pillowcase, not "as a bonus".
  • No selling anything for money. Swaps are pillow for pillow, same as the rest of the site.
  • No smoky-smelling covers. Wash it before you swap it. Ironically, smoke-free is the dream.
  • Yes to pillows and pillow covers with 420 style: leaf prints, tie-dye, smileys, lava lamps, retro rainbows and puns.
  • The jokes here are for grown-ups (21+ humor). The pillows themselves are for everyone.

Everything on this page follows the regular swap rules. Listings that break them can be reported and removed.

Frequently blazed questions

Wait, is this actually a weed exchange?

Nope. Not even a little. It is a swap for 420-themed throw pillows and couch decor. If it is not a pillow or a pillow cover, it does not belong here.

What counts as a 420 pillow?

Leaf prints, tie-dye, sleepy smileys, lava-lamp patterns, retro rainbow stripes, "couch-locked" embroidery, snack prints and anything else with a mellow, groovy vibe. Use your judgment and keep it tasteful.

Does it cost anything?

Posting and offering swaps is free. You trade one pillow for another. If you ship, you and your swap partner agree on who pays to send what.

How do people find my 420 pillow?

Put "420" in the title or description when you post it. Anyone who searches 420 on the browse page will see it.

Is Puff available for parties?

Puff is booked solid with naps through next year. Sorry.

Got a pillow that's too baked for your decor?

Pass it on to someone who will love it, and pick up something fresh. Puff, puff, pass the pillow.
