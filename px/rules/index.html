Swap rules

Pillow Exchange works because people send each other pillows they would be happy to receive. These rules keep it that way. They are short on purpose.

1. Clean, every time

  • Wash every cover before you list it, following its care label, and let it dry completely. Brand-new covers can be listed as they are.
  • No stains, mildew, musty or smoky smells, pet hair you have not removed, or signs of bed bugs, moths or other pests.
  • If you include an insert, it should be clean, dry and in good shape. If it is not, swap the cover only.
  • Pack pillows in a clean bag or box so they arrive clean.

2. Describe it honestly

  • Use your own, recent photos of the actual pillow. Show any fading, pilling, pulls or repairs.
  • Say whether the insert is included, and whether it comes from a smoke-free or pet-free home.
  • If anything changes before the swap, tell the other person.

3. What you can list

Throw pillows, cushion covers, lumbar pillows and pillow inserts. Sets are fine. Please do not list:

  • Bed pillows that have been slept on, or anything that is not clean (see rule 1).
  • Recalled products, items with loose small parts for children, or anything damaged in a way that makes it unsafe.
  • Counterfeit or knock-off branded items.
  • Anything that is not a pillow or pillow cover, for now. Other categories will come later.
  • Listings that are really ads, sales for money, or links to other shops.

4. How a swap happens

  1. You make an offer with one of your pillows. The owner accepts or declines.
  2. When an offer is accepted, both of you can see each other's email in My swaps. Agree the details there: shipping or a meetup, timing, and anything else.
  3. When you ship, each person usually pays to send their own pillow. Agree this up front and share tracking numbers.
  4. For a local meetup, choose a busy public place in daylight, and bring a friend if you like.
  5. Swaps are between members. Pillow Exchange does not inspect, hold, ship or insure items, and cannot step in on a swap that goes wrong. Only trade with someone you are comfortable with.

5. Be kind, stay safe

  • Be polite, reply promptly, and follow through on swaps you accept.
  • Never send money, gift cards or personal documents to complete a swap. Swaps are pillow for pillow.
  • Do not share your home address until you are comfortable, and never put it in a listing.
  • Use the Report this listing link on any listing that breaks these rules. Listings with several reports are hidden automatically while we look.

We may remove listings or accounts that break these rules. Questions? Contact us. See also the site terms and privacy policy.
