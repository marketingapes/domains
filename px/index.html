How a pillow swap works

  • Covers washed, every time

    Every cover is washed (or brand new) before it is listed, with no stains, odors, smoke or pests. Follow the care label, and air it fully dry before you pack it.

  • Insert optional

    Plenty of people swap just the cover and keep their own insert. If you include the insert, tick the box so the other person knows exactly what they are getting.

  • Ship it or meet up

    Choose shipping, a local meetup or either. For meetups, pick a busy public place such as a coffee shop or a library lobby. When you ship, each person pays to send their own pillow.

  • Fair, one-for-one trades

    Trades are pillow for pillow, or set for set. Say what you are hoping for in your listing, and be honest about wear, fading and any repairs.

  • You stay in control

    Nobody gets your email until you accept their offer. You can decline any offer, withdraw your own, or take a listing down whenever you like.

  • Exchange anything, next

    We are starting with throw pillows because everyone seems to have a pile of them. Once pillow swaps are running smoothly, we plan to open more home categories.

Before your first trade, read the short swap rules. They cover cleanliness, what can and cannot be listed, and how to swap safely.
