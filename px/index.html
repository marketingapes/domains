Start with how you sleep

The single biggest thing that decides your ideal pillow is the position you spend most of the night in. Each one asks something different of the pillow.

  • SideTallest pillow of all, to fill the gap between ear and mattress.
  • BackMedium height, with a little extra support under the neck curve.
  • StomachThin and soft — sometimes a flat pillow under the hips too.
  • All overAn adjustable, responsive pillow that lets you roll without a fight.

Why "sleep on it"?

A pillow is the one bit of bedding you cannot judge standing up. Height, firmness and fill behave differently after six hours than after six seconds, and your neck needs a week or two to get used to anything new. So we focus on what you can check before you buy — your sleep position, your build, your mattress — and on how to trial a pillow fairly once it arrives.

We do not rank brands or pretend to have tested products. Instead, each guide explains the principles so you can read a product description, a care label or a returns policy and know what it means for you.
