Measure the insert and cover separately

Use the same unit for every measurement. Put the pillow on a flat surface and measure its outside length and width without compressing or stretching it. Note whether your tape follows the curved surface or measures the straight edge-to-edge span; keep the method consistent.

For a removable cover, check its care instructions before removing it. Lay the empty cover flat, smooth it gently and measure the usable interior panel from seam to seam. Decorative borders, flanges and piping are not necessarily usable space for the insert.

For an envelope closure, note the overlap and usable opening. For a zipper, note the opening length. A cover may have enough interior space but still be difficult to put on a thick or shaped pillow.

Your measurements

Compare with the seller’s fit instructions

Copy the listed dimensions for the exact cover variant. Check whether those numbers describe the insert it is intended to fit or the cover itself. Labels such as “standard” or “king” are not a substitute for that distinction.

There is no single extra allowance that guarantees a fit across fabrics, fillings and closures. Use the maker’s recommended insert dimensions. If they are missing, ask the seller before choosing a size; do not infer a fit from length and width alone.

Before you choose a cover

Keep the result with the product details

When the cover arrives, compare its label with the order before washing it. Follow the care label and the seller’s return conditions. Measurements help you compare fit; they do not predict comfort or a health outcome.
