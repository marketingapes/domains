If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised based on your browsing. Where required by law (for example in the UK and European Economic Area), you will be asked for consent before non-essential cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time.

Analytics

You can learn more about how Google handles this data at the link above, and you can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
