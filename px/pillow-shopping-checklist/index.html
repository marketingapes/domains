A useful starting point

Compare pillows without the guesswork.

A practical shopping checklist focused on dimensions, materials, care and return terms.

01 · Measure what needs to fit

Check the pillow dimensions against your pillowcases and bed setup. Product names and size labels can hide differences; compare the measurements listed by each seller.

02 · Compare the construction

Note the fill material, cover material and whether the fill is adjustable. Read the care label and whether the cover can be removed. Treat comfort claims as claims, not a promise of the same experience for everyone.

03 · Read the trial conditions

A trial period and a return policy may have different conditions. Check opening, washing and hygiene exclusions, return shipping and any fees. Retain the product details and receipt for your records.
