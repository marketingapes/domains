Prom Hair and Makeup: A Planning Guide for Students and Parents

By the Hair 2 Makeup team · Last updated 23 September 2026

Prom is often the first big event where hair and makeup feel important, and it usually lands on the same evening for everyone in town. That combination means local stylists get busy, bathroom mirrors get crowded and plans change at the last minute. A little planning a few weeks ahead makes the day far more fun. This guide is written for students and for the parents helping them.

DIY, a friend, or a professional?

There is no right answer; it depends on the look, the budget and how confident you are. Here is a comparison to talk through together.

Many people mix and match: professional hair and DIY makeup is a common choice, because updos are harder to do on yourself than makeup.

Timeline: when to do what

  • Six to eight weeks out: decide on your outfit, or at least the colour and neckline. If you want a professional, start asking now because prom evenings book up locally.
  • Four weeks out: book appointments and confirm prices, deposit and timing. If you are getting a haircut or colour, schedule it roughly two weeks before prom so it has time to settle.
  • Two weeks out: practise your makeup look once, in daylight, and take a photo with flash. Collect everything you need.
  • The week of: confirm the appointment, plan transport and make sure your outfit fits with any shoes and accessories.
  • The day: eat a proper meal, allow extra time, and keep a small touch-up kit in your bag.

Matching hair to your outfit

Your neckline and the back of your outfit are the biggest clues for hair. Some general pairings people often find work well:

  • Strapless or sweetheart: almost anything works; loose waves show off the shoulders, and an updo looks elegant.
  • High neck or halter: an updo, sleek ponytail or half-up style keeps hair from hiding the neckline.
  • Open or low back: an updo or side-swept style shows off the detail.
  • Busy or embellished top: simpler hair balances it; very detailed hair can compete.

Think about comfort too. Prom involves dancing and often a warm room. If you tend to get hot, having hair off your neck, or at least a clip in your bag to put it up later, is a good idea.

Makeup that suits you and lasts the night

The best prom makeup still looks like you, just a little more polished. A few practical pointers:

  • Pick one focus. Bold eyes with a softer lip, or a statement lip with simpler eyes, usually looks more balanced than both at full volume.
  • Coordinate, don't match. Makeup does not have to be the same colour as your outfit. Neutral or complementary shades often look better in photos.
  • Test anything new. New products should be tried a week or two earlier in case of irritation, especially around the eyes.
  • Think about photos. Flash photography is common at proms. See our guide to makeup for photos and flash for tips.

A note for parents: budget and boundaries

Prom costs can climb quickly between outfit, tickets, transport, hair, makeup and nails. It helps to agree a total budget early and let the student decide how to split it. If you are booking a professional for a teenager, check who will be present at the appointment, confirm the look in advance so there are no surprises, and make sure prices are in writing. Keep hygiene in mind at group get-ready sessions: mascara, lip products and eye pencils are best not shared.

Frequently asked questions

How early should I book a prom stylist?

As soon as you know the date, ideally several weeks ahead, because many people in the same area need appointments on the same evening.

Should I get a haircut right before prom?

It is often better to have it done about two weeks before, so the cut has time to settle and there is time to fix anything you don't love.

Can I do my own prom makeup?

Yes. Practise the full look at least once beforehand, in daylight and in a flash photo, so there are no surprises on the day.

What should go in a prom touch-up bag?

Lipstick, blotting papers, a little powder, bobby pins and a phone charger cover most needs.
