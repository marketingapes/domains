Common questions

Is Sofia a person?

No. Sofia is an AI intake assistant, not a person and not an attorney. She organizes what you share; she can’t tell you whether you have a claim.

Can Sofia tell me what a case is worth?

No. Sofia doesn’t estimate value or promise review, acceptance, compensation or timing.

What if I was partly at fault?

Shared-fault rules vary by state. Choose “Shared fault” or “Not sure,” and an independent firm can review the details.
