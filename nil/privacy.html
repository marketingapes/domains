Privacy Policy

Effective August 18, 2026

Nearest Injury Lawyers (“NIL”) is a marketing and matching service operated by Marketing Apes LLC. It is not a law firm.

Information we collect

We may collect contact information, accident type, location, approximate timing, reported injury status, consent records, call or chat information, referral URL, advertising identifiers, device/browser information, and campaign attribution such as UTMs and click IDs.

How we use information

  • Operate the intake and matching service.
  • Have Sofia or another authorized provider contact you under the permission you provide.
  • Evaluate routing based on geography and participating-firm criteria.
  • Share an inquiry with participating law firms for independent review.
  • Measure advertising, prevent fraud, maintain records, and improve the service.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

AI calls, texts, and recordings

Sofia is an AI intake assistant. If you provide contact permission, NIL and authorized parties may use automated technology and prerecorded or artificial voice to call or text you. Calls may be recorded for quality, compliance, routing, and follow-up. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to an applicable text to opt out.

How information may be shared

Information may be shared with participating law firms, communications and intake vendors, hosting and analytics providers, advertising platforms for permitted measurement, fraud/security providers, professional advisers, and government or legal authorities when required. A participating firm may apply its own privacy practices after receiving an inquiry.

Advertising and analytics

We use browser and campaign signals to understand which marketing produces meaningful inquiries. We do not intentionally send detailed accident answers or narrative to advertising platforms. Browser technologies may still receive page and conversion signals according to their own policies.

Retention and security

We retain information as reasonably needed for intake, matching, compliance, dispute prevention, measurement, and legal obligations. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of information associated with you, subject to legal and operational exceptions. You can withdraw future contact permission by using the opt-out method in a message or contacting us.

Contact

Email privacy@marketingapes.com. Please do not email sensitive accident or medical details.

Updates

We may update this policy. The effective date above identifies the current version. See our Terms of Use.
