How the verdict works

The verdict is rule-based, not written by AI. It only uses prices we recorded ourselves from stores' affiliate product feeds.

  • Not enough history yet: we have seen this item on fewer than 3 days, or over less than 14 days. We show the current price and say so instead of guessing.
  • Lowest price we have recorded: today's price matches or beats every price we have logged for it.
  • Below its usual price: at least 5% under the middle (median) of the prices we logged.
  • About its usual price: within 5% of that median.
  • Higher than usual: more than 5% above it. Waiting may pay off.

When a store marks an item down, its feed often lists a regular price next to the sale price. We show that as "the store's listed regular price". It is the store's claim, and we have not checked it against past prices.

We started logging prices on Nov 15, 2026, so most items will say "not enough history yet" for the first couple of weeks. The log grows with every daily refresh. We track 1,440 products from 24 partner stores today; links to other stores will show their verified codes if we work with them.

What we never do

We do not invent "was" prices, savings percentages, ratings or reviews. If we have no data for something, the checker says so.
