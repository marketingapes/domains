Before you click

Is it really a deal?

Black Friday is when "was" prices get the most creative. Here is how we judge offers on this page, and how to check anything else you find.

Store deals get a Deal Score

Every store deal above has a 0–100 Deal Score built from the merchant's own network terms:

  • Savings size (up to 45 points). Percent off counts most. "Up to" offers count at 75% because only some items get the top discount.
  • Certainty (up to 25). Points come off for "select items", first-order-only, sign-ups, minimum spends and local-only vouchers.
  • Ease (up to 15). No code beats a code, and a code beats an email sign-up.
  • Deadline clarity (up to 15). A real end date within 45 days scores highest.

What we earn is never part of the score. Open "Network terms and score breakdown" on any card to see the math.

Amazon picks don't get a score

We don't have verified price history for Amazon listings, so we don't score them or show their prices. We picked them because they are well-known products with a long record of strong customer ratings. Before you buy, run the 60-second check:

  1. Ignore the "was" price. List prices are often a number nobody paid. Compare with what the item sold for over the past month, using a price tracker or the store's own history.
  2. Check who sells it. "Ships from and sold by Amazon.com" or the brand's own store is safer than an unknown third-party seller.
  3. Count the whole cost. Shipping, tax, and return shipping can wipe out a small discount.
  4. Check the return window. Holiday purchases often get extended returns, but not every seller offers them.

Cyber Monday questions

When is Cyber Monday 2026?

Monday, November 30, 2026, the Monday after Black Friday (Friday, November 27).

Are Cyber Monday deals better than Black Friday?

Not by default. Some stores run the same discount across the whole weekend; Peet's, for example, lists 25% off with the same code for both. Compare the Deal Score and the end time, not the name of the sale.

Why don't you show Amazon prices?

We don't have a verified live Amazon price feed, so each pick links to Amazon, where you see the current price before you buy.
