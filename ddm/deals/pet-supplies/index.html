Before you buy flea, tick or heartworm products

  • Match the weight band. Chews and spot-ons come in color-coded weight ranges; the wrong band means the wrong dose.
  • Ask your vet. Some of these products need a prescription in the US, and your vet knows your pet's history. A discount isn't a reason to switch treatments.
  • Compare per-dose cost. Pack sizes vary (1, 3, 6 or 12 doses), so divide the cart total by the number of doses.
  • Check shipping time. Order before the current dose runs out.
