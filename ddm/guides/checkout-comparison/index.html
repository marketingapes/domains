A worksheet to keep

Compare the checkout, then the return.

A two-seller worksheet for the amount due now, recurring charges and the deadlines that matter if you return an item.

Write on a printed copy or type below before printing. Entries stay in this page; nothing is submitted or saved. Closing or reloading may clear them.

Put both checkouts on the same basis

Open the exact variant and quantity you would buy from each seller. Use the same delivery destination and currency. Record the amounts shown at checkout before placing an order. Leave unknown amounts marked “unknown” instead of treating them as zero.

Amount due now = item subtotal − applied discount + shipping + tax + mandatory fees. If tax or another charge is already included in a line, do not add it twice. Copy the final checkout total as a separate cross-check.

Do not subtract an advertised coupon until it is actually applied. Keep gift-card credit, rewards and possible future rebates separate so you can distinguish the order cost from how you pay for it.

Make the decision you can explain

If quantities differ, divide the order total by comparable usable units and write down what each unit means. A cheap item you cannot use or return may not be the better purchase.

Save the seller’s product description, applied promotion and return terms with your order confirmation. This worksheet is a record of what you checked, not a seller’s promise or a return authorization.
