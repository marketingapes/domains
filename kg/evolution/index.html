A personal note · September 29, 2026

What I think AI is really for.

Not replacing people. Giving one person enough leverage to connect the whole business, measure reality, and keep improving.

Kyle Gosselin · Builder, marketer, operator

I have spent more than twenty years in marketing, and I think I finally understand what changed.

For most of my career, every part of the business lived in its own box. Google Ads over here. Facebook over there. Landing pages somewhere else. Phone calls in another system. Spreadsheets everywhere. Agencies, vendors, CRMs, reporting tools — all trying to tell you a different version of what happened.

The problem was never that we did not have enough tools.

The problem was that the tools could not see the same truth.

That is what I have been trying to solve.

Open the channels. Measure the whole path. Find the closers. Understand the assists. Cut the fat. Put the money back into what actually works.

I call the system the Evolution Engine because that is exactly what I want it to do: evolve.

Google. Meta. Instagram. TikTok. SEO. Landing pages. Forms. Chat. Voice AI. Email. SMS. Every channel can bring someone into the system, but the system should not care which platform gets the credit. It should care about what happened next.

Traffic Conversation Qualified Closed Learn

If a channel produces closers, fund it. If it consistently assists the paths that close, understand its role. If it produces neither, stop paying for it. Then test again, because markets change, creative changes, audiences change, and the system should be able to change with them.

That is also why I do not think AI should be the decision-maker sitting above the business. AI is the leverage. The business still needs rules, receipts, human judgment, and real outcomes.

Human contextWhat we know now, what matters, and what we are trying to accomplish.
Permanent dataWhat actually happened over time, not what we remember happening.
AI executionBuild, analyze, test, summarize, and move work faster than one person could before.
Outcome feedbackSigned business, revenue, and verified results decide what the next version becomes.

I started with a very simple idea: could you run a real lead-generation business with Google Drive, Sheets, BigQuery, a handful of APIs, and AI?

I think the answer is becoming yes.

Not because AI is magic. Because for the first time, one person can connect enough of the business together to see the full movie instead of a pile of screenshots.

That is what I am building now. In public. One loop at a time.

— Kyle
