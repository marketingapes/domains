Eventually he learned something more valuable than any single channel.

The channel isn’t the strategy.

The strategy is understanding the business objective, using every channel that makes sense to reach the customer, measuring what actually happened, and using that outcome to make the next decision better.

Then AI showed up.

Kyle did not treat AI like a replacement for everything he had learned. He treated it like the newest tool in the toolbox — and started asking better questions.

Can we connect this? Can we automate that? Why is a person still doing this manually? Why doesn’t marketing know which opportunity became revenue?

Spend less. Waste less. Get more. Own the system.

The goal is not “more AI.” The goal is a smarter operating model for customer acquisition.
