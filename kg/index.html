Chapter 02

The ads never learned what actually closed.

Marketing generates leads. Intake talks to people. Cases get signed. Three different rooms, and nobody ever sent back what closed.

So the ads could never learn. Every channel fought to make itself look good, and the money leaked out of the gaps between them.

“Everyone is fighting to make one channel work. The real problem is the gaps between channels.”— Me, on X

Chapter 03

Then the phone rings, and nobody answers.

Somebody gets hurt. They don't know where to turn, so they tap an ad and call. That call is the whole reason the ad exists.

And it lands at the worst possible time.

After hours

The office is closed. The person who needs help is not.

→ voicemail
Weekends

Saturday is when life happens. Monday is when somebody calls back.

→ voicemail
Call spikes

The campaign works, the lines fill up, and the best calls ring out.

→ voicemail

You already paid for that call. Letting it ring out is the most expensive thing in marketing.

Chapter 04

So I built Softía. It picks up.

Softía is the AI intake line I built at Marketing Apes. It answers when your team can't: nights, weekends, and when call volume spikes.

It doesn't replace your ads or your agency. It sits behind the media you already run and makes sure the calls you paid for actually get answered and qualified.
