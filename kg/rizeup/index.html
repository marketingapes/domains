What's wrong, line by line

Their homepage, quoted. Then what's wrong with it.

1

They sell services. I sell signed cases.

Their homepage"AI Visibility & SEO · Web Design · Paid Media · Social Media Marketing — Achieve measurable growth and visibility with our proven strategies."

Four services. "Measurable growth" — then measure it. Their hero stats: 14,000 calls delivered, 5,490 first-page rankings, 120+ five-star reviews. Calls aren't cases. Rankings aren't revenue. Reviews are vibes.

14,000 calls delivered — how many signed a retainer? They don't say. I do: $74.12 per signed.

2

Their testimonials are vibes.

Their clients"Responsive, creative, and attentive." "Professional, friendly, helpful." "The people are friendly, responsive, professional."

Every testimonial is about how nice the agency is. Not one says what the marketing did. Did anyone sign a case? What did it cost? Nobody knows.

Feelings aren't a funnel.

3

Discovery call → roadmap → "results."

Their process"Schedule a discovery call. Receive a growth roadmap. Most of our clients start seeing results within 30 days."

A roadmap is a document. It's not a machine. Nobody shows you the loop — what happens when someone clicks, who talks to them, what it costs when they sign. "Results" is never defined.

My test: $2,500, built in front of you, and I prequalify the leads live. You watch it work before you spend another dollar.

4

"AI Visibility & SEO" is not AI.

Their AI offering"Soar through the search results with our law firm SEO service."

That's their AI play: rank higher. Meanwhile nobody answers the lead at 11pm. Nobody qualifies them. Nobody transfers them live. Traffic without intake is expensive window shopping.

My AI picks up the phone. Sofia qualifies every lead in seconds and live-transfers the good ones to your desk.

5

No contracts means no accountability.

Their words"Lacking formal contracts allows us the flexibility to swiftly adapt to evolving objectives."

Translation: they never have to promise a number. If there's no number, there's no accountability. "Flexibility" is what you sell when you can't sell results.

I put my numbers on my homepage.

6

"We educate our clients."

Their words"We educate our clients on the best practices and strategies in digital marketing."

Free education — the thing I swore off years ago. Why would a firm keep paying you if you're giving away the how?

Show outcomes. Never architecture.

Their call to action"Want to See How You Stack Up Against Competitors? Gain valuable insights with our comprehensive analysis."

A free comprehensive analysis to win the job. Free work up front, vibes-based testimonials after. That's the whole business model.
