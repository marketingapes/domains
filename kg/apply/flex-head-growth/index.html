Growth is not a channel plan. It is a capital-allocation system.

A company serving business owners across self-serve and sales-led motions needs one operating model for acquisition, activation, sales progression, unit economics and learning.

20+ years digital growth$150M+ paid mediaAcquisition -> CRM -> outcomeAI leverage / human accountability

What experience changed.

At ACE, the digital function supported growth from $11M to $24M in annual online revenue. The lesson I kept: measurement matters when it changes where the next dollar and next experiment go.

What I would do first

  1. Create one growth model linking source to activation to qualified sales motion to funded customer to contribution economics.
  2. Separate platform attribution from incrementality and marginal return before scaling budgets.
  3. Attack the highest-friction handoff between self-serve and sales-led journeys first.
  4. Use AI to multiply research, creative iteration, lifecycle orchestration and analysis - not to weaken decision standards.
  5. Run experiments with pre-declared scale/stop conditions and a weekly capital-allocation review.
