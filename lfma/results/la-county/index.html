02 / From arrival to outcome

The handoff is part
of the result.

This is the reporting process to reconcile with the firm. It is not a claim that every historical record completed every step.

Explore the lead-verification demonstration ↗
  1. 01

    Keep the media receipt

    Record channel, campaign and reporting dates. Distinguish paid-platform leads from downstream intake records.

  2. 02

    Reconcile each arrival

    Count raw submissions, define duplicates and retain the source connection. One person can create more than one record.

  3. 03

    Document intake and transfer

    Separate attempted contact, intake completion and an acknowledged firm handoff. Delivery alone is not acceptance.

  4. 04

    Get the firm’s outcome

    Litify is the firm-side outcome feedback source. The firm reports acceptance and confirms executed retainers. Keep a generic “Converted” label separate until its meaning and supporting evidence are verified.

  5. 05

    Close the loop

    Connect ad spend → lead → intake → Litify outcome → optimization. Match spend to the same lead cohort before calculating costs. This report uses a firm-side export; it does not establish a live automated feedback integration or Conversions API delivery.

03 / What the review should teach us

Better evidence.
Better next decisions.

Status discipline

Make the firm’s answer explicit.

A generic converted status needs a documented definition. Confirm signed cases separately rather than treating a workflow label as proof.

Cohort discipline

Compare like with like.

Reconcile media dates with lead creation and outcome dates. A new launch and an older intake export do not form one cost-per-case calculation.

Feedback discipline

Find the missing stage.

Raw leads, unique people, handoffs and signed cases answer different questions. Missing stage counts identify a reporting task, not a campaign failure rate.

Outcome verification

The 18 “Converted” records have retainer-sent dates. Sent is not signed. No independent executed-retainer confirmation is supplied in this evidence, so confirmed signed cases and cost per confirmed signed case remain unverified. Missing confirmation is not a zero-case result.
