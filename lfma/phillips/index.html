How the Queue Works

  1. First contact. A caller reaches the intake line and speaks with Sofia, the AI intake guide. Every call is recorded, and the caller is told so up front.
  2. Qualification on the call. Sofia listens to the caller's account, confirms the facts that matter — what happened, when, where, the harm, whether they already have a lawyer — and only moves forward when the claim fits the firm's criteria.
  3. Warm transfer. With the caller's permission, Sofia connects them directly to the firm's intake team and hands off everything gathered. Complex tort matters go to the complex-tort line; vehicle accident and PI matters go to the PI/MVA line.
  4. Disposition. The firm returns its call on every transferred lead — qualified or not qualified, with the reason — and every signed matter is tied back to the lane that produced it.

Current Engine Build

The intake and delivery system serving the firm: five lanes, one AI intake line per brand, warm transfer of qualified claimants straight to the firm's intake team, delivery into Litify, and call tracking with recordings on every transferred call.

How a Lead Reaches You

  1. Sofia answers in seconds. Every call is picked up by the AI intake guide, which introduces itself and notes the call is being recorded.
  2. She listens first. The caller describes what happened in their own words. Sofia pulls out what matters — what happened, when, where, the harm — and only asks what she still needs.
  3. She qualifies on the spot. The facts are checked against the firm's criteria for the lane — for vehicle accidents: an Arizona crash, within the last two years, not the caller's fault, injury with medical care, and no lawyer already retained. The moment she knows they qualify, she moves to handoff — no extra questions. Callers who don't fit are closed kindly, with no legal opinion given.
  4. Warm transfer, live. With the caller's permission, Sofia connects them directly to the firm's intake team and hands off what she gathered — name, number, and the basics of the claim.
  5. Delivered into Litify. Lead details flow into the firm's Litify system with the source lane attached, and every transferred call is tracked and recorded end to end.
