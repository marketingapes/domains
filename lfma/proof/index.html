Define the work

Start with one customer journey.

In a workflow review, we identify where the inquiry starts, the permission it carries, who receives it and how the outcome returns. We agree the acceptance criteria before implementation so both teams can see whether the work delivered.

Useful acceptance criteria include a duplicate-safe receipt, a correctly routed inquiry, a working opt-out, and a buyer outcome joined to the original lead. Your scope, responsibilities and fee are agreed before an invoice is issued.

Historical campaign summaries are reviewed separately. A source-system status or a sent retainer is not automatically proof of a signed agreement, a collected fee or a fully attributed acquisition cost.
