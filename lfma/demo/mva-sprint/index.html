01 / The operating idea

Case fit first.
Intake ownership next.

Agree on the desired MVA case profile before testing demand. Prequalification gathers information; your firm makes the legal and acceptance decisions.

  1. 01

    Earn the attention.

    Test whether the market responds to an approved MVA message. Compare firm-branded and unbranded concepts without assuming either wins.

    AD → PAGE
  2. 02

    Offer a way in.

    Test traditional form, call-first and conversational intake. Compare completion and intake quality alongside the work each route asks of your team.

    PAGE → INTAKE
  3. 03

    Ask. Explain. Get consent.

    Capture incident timing, jurisdiction, injury context and representation status. Explain the next step and obtain consent; attorneys assess eligibility.

    INTAKE → CONSENT
  4. 04

    Make the handoff explicit.

    Give the intake owner source, context and consent status. Test staff availability, declined connections and follow-up ownership before going live.

    CONSENT → HANDOFF
  5. 05

    Close the measurement loop.

    Reconcile spend with accepted intake and verified signed outcomes. Learn whether friction lives in demand, response time, case fit or the handoff.

    HANDOFF → EVIDENCE

01 / MARKET

Does the message
produce relevant demand?

Review response by creative, identity and source. Compare inquiry quality against your agreed MVA profile, not just low-cost clicks.

02 / FIRM

Can your intake team
convert that demand?

Trace response time, connection attempts, staff acceptance and follow-up. Tie signed outcomes back to the originating inquiry.

03 / PROCESS

Where does AI
earn its place?

Compare the information gathered and work required across intake routes. Test disclosure, consent and human fallback before making an AI claim.

What your team receives / proposed intake packet

Source + route. Campaign and creative context, branded or unbranded entry and intake method.

Prequalification + consent. Reported incident details, disclosure status, connection permission and unanswered questions.

Owner + disposition. Intake destination, acknowledgement, next action and eventual verified signed outcome.
