Four Pillars of Law Firm SEO

Ranking in competitive legal markets requires a comprehensive approach. We cover all four pillars simultaneously.

Technical SEO

The foundation. If your site is slow, broken, or poorly structured, nothing else matters. We fix the technical issues that are holding your rankings back.

  • Site speed optimization (Core Web Vitals)
  • Mobile responsiveness audit
  • Crawlability and indexation fixes
  • Schema markup for legal services
  • XML sitemap and robots.txt optimization
  • Internal linking structure
  • HTTPS and security audit

On-Page SEO

Every page on your site is a ranking opportunity. We optimize your content, metadata, and page structure to signal relevance and authority to search engines.

  • Practice area page optimization
  • Title tags and meta descriptions
  • Header hierarchy and content structure
  • Keyword mapping and density
  • Image optimization and alt text
  • Content gap analysis
  • Conversion optimization (CRO) integration

Local SEO

For most law firms, local search is where the cases come from. "Lawyer near me" searches have exploded. We make sure your firm dominates the local map pack.

  • Google Business Profile optimization
  • NAP consistency across directories
  • Local citation building
  • Review generation strategy
  • Local content creation
  • Service area page development
  • Map pack ranking optimization

Content & Link Building

Content drives rankings and links drive authority. We create legal content that answers real questions and build high-quality backlinks that move the needle.

  • Legal blog content strategy and writing
  • Practice area pillar pages
  • FAQ and educational content
  • Digital PR and link outreach
  • Legal directory submissions
  • Guest posting on legal publications
  • Competitor backlink analysis
