      A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

      Do I keep my domain and brand?

      Yes. Your domain keeps its own identity. Ownership, account access, deliverables, and responsibilities are agreed in your scope.

      Can you run my ads too?

      Yes—campaign operation can be part of the engagement, alongside landing pages, lead response, and reporting. We agree on platforms, access, creative, and spend before campaigns run. Media spend is separate from service fees unless your agreement says otherwise.

      Can you work with my existing tools?

      We start by reviewing what works. The plan identifies the tools to connect, any limitations, and what actually needs replacing.

      Does Sofia replace every human?

      No. Sofia handles a defined role. Your operation includes human decisions, escalation rules, and an accountable owner.

      How much does it cost?

      It depends on the domain, workflow, campaign scope, and ongoing support. Start with the planner; Kyle will confirm scope and pricing before anything is purchased or launched.

      Is the starter plan an AI consultation?

      It is an immediate guided outline based on your selections, not a live AI analysis or a guarantee. We review your business and existing systems before making an implementation recommendation.
