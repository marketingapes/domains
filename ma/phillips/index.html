If the budget runs out first

My proposed approach: pause new paid activity at the approved cap, review the outcomes and decide together whether to stop, change scope or approve more funding.

  • No automatic budget extension.
  • Spending stops at the agreed cap, even if the goal has not been reached.
  • Any additional work or funding needs a separate agreement.
