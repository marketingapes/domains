Business workflow review

Make one business workflow easier to run.

A focused walkthrough with Kyle Gosselin: bring a real process, identify the handoffs, and decide what is worth improving.

01 · Start with one outcome

Choose a concrete task: responding to an inquiry, moving a listing into publication, or assembling a campaign report. Bring an example that can be discussed without exposing customer information.

02 · Map what actually happens

List the systems involved, who owns each step, and what happens when a step fails. A useful plan distinguishes work that can be automated from decisions that need a person.

03 · Agree how to judge progress

Choose a baseline, a deliverable and a review date. Define what success would look like before work starts. Any paid implementation follows an agreed scope and price.
