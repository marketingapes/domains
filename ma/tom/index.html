What I figured out

For years the missing piece was feedback. I could buy the traffic, but I rarely heard back whether a lead signed. Without that, budget decisions are guesses. So the system is built around the firm’s own outcome: each inquiry keeps its source, its intake conversation and its follow-up, so the next decision is based on what actually signed. I’ve applied this method before, including campaigns in LA County.

What it’s meant to do

  • Turn a firm and case-type request into a branded campaign without a chain of handoffs.
  • Screen callers with Sofia, an AI intake assistant, using the firm’s questions.
  • Feed the firm’s results back into where the budget goes next.

Where it can go

The bigger picture is a firm owning its own integrated marketing and intake system. AI runs the routine workflows; the firm’s managers oversee performance and make the human decisions. That’s the direction, not what’s running today, and it doesn’t replace anyone’s staff.
