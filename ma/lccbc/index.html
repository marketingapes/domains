What Kyle created

One engine connecting the first click to the next conversation—and the outcome after it.

Verification is one piece. Kyle is also building around the people a firm doesn’t take today. With their permission, the intended workflow retains context from the conversation, supports follow-up, and flags when a fresh human review may make sense.

Not ready today does not always mean no future opportunity. A mismatch for one firm does not automatically mean there is no fit elsewhere. The goal is continuity from first inquiry onward, rather than forcing an immediate conversion.

Kyle’s offer covers marketing, intake, follow-up, source quality and measurement. The firm handles legal review and decides whether to accept a case.

An illustrative journey

One person.
A connected path.

This is the journey Kyle is building toward. Ongoing continuity and fresh-review capabilities are intended features, not verified by the public demo.

01

Create the campaign

A firm’s agreed market and criteria shape the creative and landing experience.

02

Start a conversation

The person requests contact. An AI intake guide explains its role and gathers the agreed information.

03

Add the right checks

Check whether the traffic matches the campaign and intended area. Preserve source and interaction history, investigate mismatches, and improve the source. Location signals are approximate; they do not prove identity.

04

Keep a permitted path open

With permission, retain context and support follow-up. A change in circumstances or firm fit can prompt fresh human review—not an automatic eligibility decision.

05

Handoff with context

With appropriate permission, the firm’s team receives the relevant summary. Contact details and conversation data are not transferred to another firm without that permission.

06

Learn from the outcome

Use verified quality outcomes and appropriate, permitted conversion signals to improve which traffic a campaign pursues. Sensitive claim details stay out of advertising-platform payloads. This feedback loop is intended; it is not fully live-verified.

The thread that matters: what the person requested, what they have already shared, what they permitted, and what should happen next.

The Evolution Engine is not a proven fraud or identity verifier. Verification capability, data permissions and any partner integration would require their own agreed scope and validation.
