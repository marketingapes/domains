Questions people ask

Does it cost anything?

No. The case review is free. If Phillips Law Group takes your case, there’s no attorney fee unless they win. Ask about how case costs are handled in your fee agreement.

You don’t have to give the other driver’s insurance a recorded statement. Many people speak with a lawyer first.

Arizona generally gives injured people two years to file, and some claims have shorter deadlines. Call and we’ll tell you where you stand.
