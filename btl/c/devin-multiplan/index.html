Start with the billing picture

The question is not only
what was paid.

It is how the payment relates to the services billed, the applicable arrangements and the records behind it.

Questions about MultiPlan-related payments can begin with a provider’s commercial out-of-network billing. A payment difference alone does not establish an improper underpayment or a legal claim.

The purpose of an initial conversation is to understand your organization’s circumstances and whether a more detailed review may be appropriate. Any legal assessment depends on the facts, relevant agreements and applicable law.

Understand your billing

Keep the terms clear.

Billed charges
The amount your organization billed for services.
Allowed amount
The amount identified as allowable in the relevant payment or benefit determination.
Payment received
The amount actually paid. Payment records and explanations help put that number in context.

These are general descriptions, not a conclusion about any particular payment.

Before you begin

Good questions.
Clear expectations.

Does this screen tell me whether I qualify?

No. It helps organize a possible conversation. The firm must review the facts and decide whether further evaluation is appropriate. No recovery or other outcome is promised.

What if my annual billing is near $1 million, or I am unsure?

Choose the closest answer. “Near $1 million,” “below that range” and “not sure” all have a path to clarification. The figure refers to annual commercial out-of-network billed charges, not the amount paid or a potential claim value.

Should I send medical records or patient details?

No. Do not provide patient names, medical records or other patient-level information on an initial landing page. If records are needed later, the firm should explain an approved secure process.

What can I prepare for a future conversation?

Start with a general description of your organization, the years of commercial out-of-network billing and an estimate of annual billed charges. A reviewer may later ask which payment explanations or agreements are relevant. Do not upload them here.

Can I book an appointment on this page?

Not yet. The proposed flow lets people who pre-qualify schedule directly or choose an AI scheduling call. This is a demo; neither option is connected.
