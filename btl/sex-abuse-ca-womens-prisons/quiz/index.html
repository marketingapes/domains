    Before you start

    Do I have to describe what happened?

    No. Sofia will never ask you to describe what happened. The questions are about basics like the type of place, a rough timeframe and whether you already have a lawyer. You can skip any question.

    Is there a deadline?

    Timing rules for these claims are being reviewed with counsel, and they can depend on the facts. We don’t state deadlines here. An independent law firm can explain what may apply to you.

    I’m still incarcerated or on parole. Can I still reach out?

    Yes. Anyone can call Sofia’s line or have someone they trust request a callback. You choose what to share and when.

    Is Best Tort Lawyers a law firm?

    No. Best Tort Lawyers is a matching and directory service. It does not give legal advice, and using this page or talking with Sofia does not create an attorney-client relationship.

    Is Sofia a person?

    No. Sofia is an AI intake guide, not a person and not an attorney. She can’t tell you whether you have a claim. You can ask for a person at any time.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. Talking with Sofia and requesting a review are free. We never estimate what a matter is worth or promise any result.

    Is this confidential? Where do my answers go?

    Your answers stay with Best Tort Lawyers’ intake and, only with your permission, the independent law firm that reviews these matters. They are not sold. This page measures visits with Google Tag Manager, but your answers and contact details are never sent to it.

    If you’d like support right now, the RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline is free, confidential and open 24/7: 1-800-656-4673. RAINN is not affiliated with Best Tort Lawyers. In an emergency, call 911.
