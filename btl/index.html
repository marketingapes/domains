Fair questions

Know what this is before you start.

Is Best Tort Lawyers a law firm?

No. Best Tort Lawyers is a matching and intake service. It does not provide legal advice, does not form an attorney-client relationship, and does not guarantee that any lawyer will accept a matter.

Is Sofia a lawyer?

No. Sofia is an automated AI intake assistant. She helps gather information and organize the intake. She does not give legal advice.

Do I need to know what kind of claim I have?

No. You can start by describing what happened. The intake is designed to help identify the relevant category without requiring legal terminology.

Does starting the intake mean I qualify?

No. The intake may help identify whether you may potentially qualify for further review. Any independent law firm makes its own decision about whether to evaluate or accept a matter.

Can I call instead of using chat?

Yes. You can call Sofia at (202) 932-9700 or request a callback.

What happens to my information?

Your information is used to operate the intake and, where appropriate and with applicable consent, support review and matching. See the Privacy Policy and Terms for more detail.
