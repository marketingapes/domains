Common Questions After Submitting

Who will be calling me?
A trauma-informed intake specialist working with the sponsoring law firm will reach out, usually within 24 business hours. If you miss the call, they will try again — you won't lose your place by not answering the first time.
Is my information really confidential?
Yes. What you submitted is shared only with the legal intake team reviewing your potential claim. It is not made public by this form, and it is not sold to third-party marketers.
Does submitting this form create an attorney-client relationship?
No. Submitting the form does not create an attorney-client relationship. That relationship is only formed if you choose to move forward and sign a written fee agreement — and you are never required to do so.
What if I don't have documentation of the abuse?
Documentation is not required to start a review. Many survivors begin with only their personal account. The intake team can also explain how detention and placement records may be requested later.
Is there a deadline?
California law has expanded the time survivors of childhood sexual abuse have to come forward, but deadlines depend on individual circumstances and are determined by counsel. Completing your review conversation promptly is the best way to protect your options.
