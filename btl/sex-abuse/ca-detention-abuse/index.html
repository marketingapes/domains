The $4 Billion LA County Settlement: What Victims Need to Know

Los Angeles County has agreed to a tentative $4 billion settlement — one of the largest institutional sexual abuse settlements in U.S. history — covering victims abused at county juvenile halls, probation camps, and foster care facilities. Allegations span 1985 through recent years.

The $4 billion settlement is a landmark moment — but the abuse was never limited to one county. Survivors held at juvenile detention facilities and juvenile halls across California, from San Bernardino County to Alameda, Orange, and Sacramento counties, have come forward with strikingly similar reports of abuse by staff inside county-run facilities.
