Settlement Update

The $4 Billion LA County Settlement: What Victims Need to Know

Los Angeles County has agreed to a tentative $4 billion settlement — one of the largest institutional sexual abuse settlements in U.S. history — covering victims abused at county juvenile halls, probation camps, and foster care facilities. Allegations span 1985 through recent years.

For decades, survivors of sexual abuse at LA County's probation facilities and foster care system have come forward with reports of systemic abuse at facilities including the now-closed MacLaren Hall (MacLaren Children's Center), Los Padrinos Juvenile Hall, Barry J. Nidorf Juvenile Hall, and numerous probation camps operated by the LA County Probation Department.

California's extended statute of limitations gives many survivors the ability to file claims regardless of when the abuse occurred. No police report, prior conviction, or documentation is required. The claims window may be closing — act now.
