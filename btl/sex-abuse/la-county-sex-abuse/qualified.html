Incident Details

Facility & Placement Records

Common Questions After Submitting

How soon will an attorney contact me?
A Phillips Law Group attorney will reach out within 24 business hours of receiving your submission. If you submitted on a Friday evening or weekend, expect a call by Monday morning. You can also call us directly at (602) 222-2222.
Does submitting this form create an attorney-client relationship?
No. Submitting this form does not create an attorney-client relationship. That relationship is only formed once you and your attorney have reviewed your case and signed a written fee agreement.
What if I don't have documentation of the abuse?
Documentation is not required to start a claim. Many survivors have successfully filed claims based solely on their personal accounts. Our attorneys are experienced in building cases without complete records.
Is there a deadline to file a claim?
California's extended statute of limitations for childhood sexual abuse claims allows many survivors to file regardless of how long ago the abuse occurred. However, filing windows may be limited — acting quickly ensures you don't miss the opportunity.
How much could my claim be worth?
Every case is different. The LA County settlement totals $4 billion, and individual compensation depends on the nature and severity of the abuse, documentation available, and other factors. Your attorney will discuss realistic expectations during your consultation.
