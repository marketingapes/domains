Settlement update

The $4 billion LA County settlement: what survivors should know

On April 29, 2025, the LA County Board of Supervisors approved a $4 billion settlement covering nearly 7,000 claims of sexual abuse at county juvenile halls, probation camps and foster care placements, with abuse alleged as far back as 1959.

For decades, survivors have reported abuse at facilities run or supervised by the county, including the now-closed MacLaren Hall (MacLaren Children’s Center) in El Monte, Los Padrinos Juvenile Hall, Barry J. Nidorf Juvenile Hall and the county’s probation camps.

Most of those claims were filed during a temporary window California opened for childhood sexual abuse survivors. If you didn’t file then, you may still have options — California’s rules on timing are complex and depend on the facts, and only a licensed attorney can tell you what applies. A free review is the way to find out. No outcome is guaranteed.
