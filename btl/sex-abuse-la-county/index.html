Before you start

Do I have to describe what happened?

No. Sofia will never ask you to describe what happened. The questions are about basics like the type of place, a rough timeframe and whether you already have a lawyer. You can skip any question.

It happened many years ago. Is it too late?

Not necessarily. California changed its laws on childhood sexual abuse claims, and some people may still have options years later. Phillips Law Group’s attorneys review timing under its own criteria — we can’t promise any result.

What if I’m not sure it was in LA County?

That’s okay. Choose the closest answer or “Prefer not to say.” Sofia never needs details to get started.

Is Phillips Law Group a law firm?

Yes. Phillips Law Group is a law firm. This page does not give legal advice, and using this page or talking with Sofia does not create an attorney-client relationship — that requires the firm’s acceptance and a separate written agreement.

Is Sofia a person?

No. Sofia is an AI intake assistant, not a person and not an attorney. She can’t tell you whether you have a claim. You can ask for a person at any time.

Does it cost anything?

No. Talking with Sofia and requesting a review are free. We never estimate what a matter is worth or promise any result.

Is this confidential? Where do my answers go?

Your answers stay with Phillips Law Group’s intake team. They are not sold. This page measures visits with Google Tag Manager, but your answers and contact details are never sent to it.

If you’d like support right now, the RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline is free, confidential and open 24/7: 1-800-656-4673. RAINN is not affiliated with Phillips Law Group. In an emergency, call 911.
