Common questions

Is my consultation confidential?

Yes. The consultation is free and confidential. You decide how much to share, and you can stop at any time.

How much does it cost?

Nothing to talk with us. We work on a contingency fee basis, so there is no legal fee until we win.

What happens when I call?

Our intake line answers every day, including over the holidays. Sofia, our AI intake assistant, asks a few simple questions, and a member of our legal team follows up privately to explain your options.

Do I have to describe what happened?

No. You can share as little or as much as you are comfortable with. A name and a way to reach you is enough to start.

Is there a deadline to file?

Time limits apply, and they depend on your situation. California AB 250 opened a limited window, which closes December 31, 2027, for some survivors whose claims had expired. Eligibility depends on the facts, so do not wait to talk to an attorney.

Is there an office near me?

We have 16 offices across California, from Santa Rosa to Palm Desert, and we can also talk by phone.
