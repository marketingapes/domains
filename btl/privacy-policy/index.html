Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Best Tort Lawyers ("we," "us") connects people who may have legal claims with law firm intake teams. We are not a law firm. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it, in plain language.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email address, and details about your potential claim when you call, chat, text, or submit a form.
  • Information collected automatically: device and browser details, pages you visit, and how you interact with this site — collected through cookies and measurement tools (Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta and TikTok pixels) so we can operate the site and measure our advertising.

How we use it

  • To connect you with law firm intake teams about your potential claim.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve this website.
  • To measure and improve our advertising.

Who we share it with

We share your contact information and claim details with law firms and their intake teams so they can evaluate your potential claim and contact you. We do not sell your personal information to data brokers. We may also share limited data with service providers who help us run this site (hosting, analytics, messaging), who may only use it for that purpose.

Contact consent

By submitting your information, you agree that Best Tort Lawyers and the law firm we connect you with may contact you by call, text, or email about your potential claim, including with automated technology. Consent is not a condition of any service. See our SMS Terms for text-message details.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to opt out of text messages.
  • Email Sofia@besttortlawyers.com or call (202) 932-9700 to ask what we have about you or to request deletion.
  • You can block cookies in your browser settings; some site features may not work as well.

Children

This site is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new "last updated" date.
