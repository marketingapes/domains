Good questions

What people ask us

Does it cost anything?

No. Checking is free, and the review is free. Disability lawyers are generally paid only if your claim is approved, with the fee limited by federal law.

Is this the government?

No. Best Tort Lawyers is a private service and is not connected to the Social Security Administration. You can always apply directly with Social Security for free at ssa.gov.

Is Best Tort Lawyers a law firm?

No. We connect people with participating law firms. We don’t give legal advice, and using this page doesn’t make anyone your lawyer.

When will someone call me?

At the time you pick. “As soon as possible” on a weekday between 9:30 AM and 6 PM Eastern usually means within minutes; at night or on a weekend, she calls the next business day.

Who calls me?

Sofia, our AI intake assistant. She says she’s an AI at the start of every call, and you can ask for a person anytime. If it fits, she connects you with a disability intake team, but only after you say yes.

Where does my information go?

To Best Tort Lawyers and, if your answers fit, to the participating law firm that reviews your situation. It is not sold.
