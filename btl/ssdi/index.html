What SSDI is

Social Security Disability Insurance pays monthly benefits to people who can’t work because of a medical condition expected to last at least a year, and who paid into Social Security while they worked. The Social Security Administration decides every claim.

A condition that stops work

The condition has to keep you from working and be expected to last 12 months or longer. Regular care from a doctor helps document it.

You paid into it

SSDI is insurance you paid for through payroll taxes. How much work history you need depends on your age.

Help with the process

Many first applications are denied. A disability lawyer can help with the application and with appeals, and is usually paid only if you win.
