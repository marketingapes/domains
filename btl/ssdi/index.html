Why people get help with SSDI

Social Security Disability pays monthly benefits to people who can’t work because of a health condition and who paid into Social Security while they worked.

The process is hard

Many first applications are denied. A disability lawyer knows what Social Security looks for and can help with the application and any appeal.

No upfront cost

Disability lawyers are generally paid only if you’re approved, and their fee is limited by federal law.

Your doctor’s care matters

Regular treatment helps show how your condition keeps you from working. That’s why we ask about it.

General information only, not legal or medical advice. Source: Social Security Administration, ssa.gov/disability.

What to have ready for an SSDI claim

Useful whether or not you check with us. Social Security asks for these.

  1. Names, addresses and phone numbers of your doctors, clinics and hospitals
  2. Dates of your visits and the tests or treatment you’ve had
  3. A list of your medicines and who prescribed them
  4. Your jobs over the last 5 years and the date you stopped working

Source: Social Security Administration, ssa.gov/disability.
