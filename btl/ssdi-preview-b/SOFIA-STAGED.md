# Sofia SSDI — Version B staged specification

Offline reference only. No saved Vapi assistant, phone binding, call, recording, transfer, provider write, or AI inference is active. The page uses a fixed six-message text script. It is not a live bot.

The guide identifies itself as AI, confirms SSDI context, then illustrates verify-only checks: who the inquiry is for; expected duration of at least 12 months or terminal condition; work-history review for applications or a prior denial for appeals; existing representation. No diagnosis detail, treatment history, precise dates or earnings are collected. Every scenario routes to human review. Work-credit rules vary by age; current work does not automatically decide eligibility. Appeals need prompt human review of the actual notice and circumstances, never an automated deadline verdict.

A future approved live flow must disclose any recording before intake and obtain appropriate permission, then collect only approved minimum contact fields and permission to connect. This preview collects none. Transfer destination, assistant/phone IDs and receiving team remain null. No number is borrowed from Paraquat or Phillips.

The original brief's phrase “It looks like you may qualify” is deliberately superseded here by “Next step: human review.” Health/work responses must not decide eligibility, benefits, representation or firm acceptance. This is an explicit safety exception to the historical script, not an unreported missing feature.

Applications are the default demo assumption; appeals are a separate example branch. Nationwide is example geography, never a firm's confirmed coverage. No firm-specific criteria are invented.
