How it works

Three short stages.

Sofia organizes what you already know. She does not decide eligibility, diagnose anything or promise that a firm will review or accept a matter.

01

Share the basics

One question at a time: the product, how and where contact happened, approximate years, any diagnosis a clinician gave you, and whether a lawyer already handles it. “Not sure” is always an answer.

02

Independent firm review

Questions

Before you start.

Is Best Tort Lawyers a law firm?

No. Best Tort Lawyers is a matching and directory service. It is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. Using this page or speaking with Sofia does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Is Sofia a person or a lawyer?

Sofia is an AI intake guide. She is not a person and not an attorney. She can organize the facts you share but cannot tell you whether you have a claim.

I used a weed killer but I'm not sure it was Paraquat.

That's fine. Choose “Not sure”. Sofia records what you know and never assumes that a weed killer was Paraquat.

Can Sofia tell me if I qualify or what a case is worth?

No. Sofia does not determine eligibility, estimate value or promise acceptance, compensation or timing. An independent law firm would apply its own criteria.

I already have a lawyer for this.

You can say so. Sofia will note it and will not encourage you to change lawyers.

Is my information being sent anywhere?

Not from this page yet. Online submission is not connected, so answers stay in your browser and are not stored or shared. This page loads no advertising or analytics trackers.
