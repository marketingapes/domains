The numbers that matter

Billed, allowed and paid are three different numbers

Every out-of-network claim has a billed charge, an allowed amount and a payment. When the allowed amount lands far below what you billed, that's the gap worth asking about.

A gap is a question to review, not proof of an underpayment or a claim.
