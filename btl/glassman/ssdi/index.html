Does it cost anything?

No. Checking is free, and the review is free. Disability lawyers are generally paid only if your claim is approved, with the fee limited by federal law.

Is this the government?

No. Jeffrey Glassman Injury Lawyers is a private law firm and is not connected to the Social Security Administration. You can always apply directly with Social Security for free at ssa.gov.

Who reviews my situation?

The disability team at Jeffrey Glassman Injury Lawyers, One International Place, Boston. Using this page doesn’t make us your lawyer; that happens only if the firm accepts your case and you sign an agreement.
