04 · How review works

The planned hand-off, step by step.

Screening collects information. It never decides eligibility or representation; your firm makes every legal and case-acceptance decision.

  1. Step 1

    Someone reaches out

    Through one of the three paths, with consent to be contacted.

  2. Step 2

    Screening checks fit

    The quiz or Sofia asks the agreed questions, including age.

  3. Step 3

    A named reviewer looks

    Each lead is reviewed before hand-off. Who reviews, and how fast, is being confirmed.

  4. Step 4

    Your intake decides

    Your team receives the lead and its context, and takes it from there.

What we will measure

  • Cost per delivered lead that matches your criteria
  • Cost per lead your intake team actually reaches
  • Signed cases, as reported by your team

What we won’t promise

  • A number of leads or cases
  • A cost per lead or cost per case
  • Any outcome for a claimant
