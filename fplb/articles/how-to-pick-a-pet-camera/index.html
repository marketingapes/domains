How to Pick a Pet Camera: 4 Things That Actually Matter

By the For Pets Like Blue team · Updated October 6, 2026 · 3 min read

Buying a pet camera? Ignore everything on the box. Check these four things.

1. Two-way audio that actually works

The whole point is talking to your dog when you're not home. If the speaker sounds like a drive-thru intercom, skip it.

2. Treat tossing

Gimmick? A little. But it's the difference between your dog noticing the camera and your dog loving the camera.

3. Night vision

Most of the day you're gone is... the day. But the weird stuff happens at 9pm. You want to see it.

4. Smart alerts

A camera that pings you every time a curtain moves is a camera you mute by Friday. You want person and pet detection, not motion detection.

The 20-minute homework trick

Here's the shortcut: before you buy anything, ask AI to compare the top five pet cameras. Tell it to ignore sponsored reviews and weight durability complaints over star counts. Twenty minutes of homework you were never going to do yourself, done in two.
