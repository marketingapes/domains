A useful starting point

Choose a pet portrait from the photo you have.

Write down photo quality, size, destination and return terms before you pick a style. This is a brief, not a product test and not a health claim.

01 · Use a photo the maker can actually print

Open the seller’s current photo guidelines on their site. Check lighting, crop, resolution and whether they accept screenshots. A phone photo can work; a blurry zoom usually does not. If the guidelines are missing, ask the seller before you pay.

02 · Match size and destination to the room

Write the wall or shelf size and the shipping country you would use. Confirm the product variant, frame options and stated shipping destination on the merchant page. Do not treat a homepage claim as a packed order.

03 · Read the exit before the style

Check the return window, who pays return shipping, and whether a custom print is final sale. Save the product page and the terms that influenced the order.
