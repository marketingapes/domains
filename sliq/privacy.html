Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Smart Life Insurance Quote ("we," "us," or "our"), operated by First Benefit Solutions, respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you visit smartlifeinsurancequote.com, submit a quote request, use our chat assistant, or call us.

Information We Collect

When you request a quote, use our chat, or call us, we may collect:

  • Contact information — name, phone number, email address
  • Location information — ZIP code and state
  • Insurance-related preferences — coverage type, age range, coverage amount, tobacco use, and who the coverage is for
  • Communications — call recordings (where permitted and disclosed), chat transcripts, and text messages
  • Technical data — IP address, device and browser type, referring URL, cookies, and campaign parameters (such as UTM tags and click identifiers)

How We Use Your Information

  • To connect you with licensed insurance agents who can provide quote options in your state
  • To contact you by phone, text message, or email regarding your quote request, including via automated technology where you have provided consent
  • To personalize your experience and remember your progress
  • To measure and improve our website, advertising, and marketing effectiveness
  • To detect fraud and comply with legal, regulatory, and insurance-industry requirements

How We Share Information

We share your information with licensed insurance agents, agencies, and carriers for the purpose of fulfilling your quote request. We also use service providers who process data on our behalf — such as web hosting, analytics (Google Analytics), advertising platforms (Meta), call tracking (CallRail), customer-relationship and dialing systems, and workflow tools (such as Zapier) that route your request to our intake team.

We do not sell your personal information to unrelated third-party marketers. California residents and residents of other states with privacy laws can exercise their rights on our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page.

Cookies, Analytics & Advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies (including Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, and the Meta pixel) to understand how visitors use our site and to measure advertising. These tools may collect device identifiers and browsing information. You can control cookies through your browser settings; ad platforms offer their own opt-outs (see Meta Ad Preferences and Google Ad Settings).

Telephone & Text Communications (TCPA)

By submitting a form on this site with your phone number, you consent to be contacted by Smart Life Insurance Quote, a service of First Benefit Solutions, and/or licensed insurance partners at the number provided, including by call, text message, or prerecorded/artificial voice, even if your number is on a Do Not Call list. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts, or tell any agent you wish not to be contacted again.

Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information for as long as needed to serve you, meet legal and insurance-regulatory obligations, and maintain business records. We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including encryption in transit (SSL/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your Choices & Rights

  • Opt out of sale/sharing and request access or deletion on our Do Not Sell or Share page
  • Opt out of texts by replying STOP; opt out of calls by telling any agent or contacting us below
  • Manage cookies in your browser and ad preferences with the platforms linked above

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent version. Material changes will be posted on this page.
