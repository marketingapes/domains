Business workflow review

Give the next campaign a clear handoff.

Turn a campaign idea into a brief that a person or an AI-assisted workflow can execute and review.

01 · Define the action

Write down the audience, the offer and the one action a visitor should take. Identify the destination page and the person responsible for responding.

02 · Prepare the operating brief

Include approved copy, available assets, channels, timing and a spending limit if paid media is involved. State which changes need approval and where the current version is kept.

03 · Close with a review

Record what was published, where it appeared and what it cost. Compare inquiries and accepted outcomes separately. A platform activity count alone does not establish revenue.

Bring these four things

Plan a campaign handoff

Email a short description of your business process. Do not include passwords, customer records or confidential information. Scope, price and timing are agreed separately.
