Before you start

Is Best Tort Lawyers a law firm?

No. Best Tort Lawyers is a matching and directory service. It does not give legal advice, and using this page or talking with Sofia does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Is Sofia a person?

No. Sofia is an AI intake guide, not a person and not an attorney. She organizes what you share; she can't tell you whether you have a claim.

I'm not sure the product was Paraquat.

That's fine — choose “Not sure.” Sofia never assumes a weed killer was Paraquat.

Can Sofia tell me what a case is worth?

No. Sofia doesn't estimate value or promise review, acceptance, compensation or timing.
