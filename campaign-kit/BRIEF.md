# READ FIRST — BTL campaign kit brief

This is the one brief for the BTL campaign build. Every AI seat (Claude, Codex, ChatGPT/Dot, Muse, Grok) and Kyle work from this file. Read it before you touch anything. If what you are about to do is not listed under your seat, ask Kyle first.

**Scope:** one domain (besttortlawyers.com), one generator (`campaign-kit/build.mjs`, from PR #359 and its follow-up). There are older page builders (`campaign-system/`, `btl/*/build_pages.py`). Do not extend them, and do not start a new one. New work is a new config in `campaign-kit/campaigns/` or a change to `build.mjs`.

## Rules for every seat

1. **Previews never touch production.** A config is a preview unless `status: "live"`, and the build refuses `live` until every gate below has signed evidence. Preview pages have no GTM, no TrustedForm, no phone dialing and no webhook, and their security policy is `connect-src 'none'`. CI checks this in a real browser (`tests/campaign-kit.browser.cjs`).
2. **A lead is "received" only with a receipt.** The page shows success only when the intake webhook answers 2xx with `{status: received|accepted|duplicate, receipt_id}`. The intake scenario must store the lead before it answers.
3. **Transfer destinations are read from Vapi, never assumed.** `delivery.transfer` records the number Sofia's transfer tool actually dials, its kind (`firm` / `internal` / `demo`) and where it was read. A Sofia line can never transfer to itself. Live transfer needs kind `firm`.
4. **Nothing is "working" without evidence.** Checklist rows come from `evidence` and `gates` in each config, with a source and date. Anything without evidence shows UNTESTED or MISSING. Do not mark something WORKING because it should work.
5. **No spend, no sends.** Ad drafts stay paused. Activation, invoices and messages to firms or claimants need Kyle's approval in chat, naming the exact thing.
6. **Never invent buyer criteria.** Criteria come from the buyer in writing (gate `criteria_approved`).
7. **Change by PR.** Run `node campaign-kit/build.mjs --all`, `node --test campaign-kit/test/*.test.mjs` and `node tests/campaign-kit.browser.cjs`. Then open a PR. Do not push to main.
