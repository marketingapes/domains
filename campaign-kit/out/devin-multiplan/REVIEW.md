## Review and connection gates
- Firm approval of copy, disclosures, educational material, scope and final screening criteria.
- Confirm consultation terms, permitted geography and final pre-qualification criteria, plus direct self-scheduling and the optional AI scheduling call. Neither scheduling route is connected.
- Approved privacy/consent language and separate, optional educational-email permission.
- Connect approved intake only after durable receipt, failure and duplicate tests pass.
- Verify campaign identity, analytics and platform permissions before any advertising.
