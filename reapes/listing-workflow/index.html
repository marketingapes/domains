Business workflow review

Publish a listing with fewer loose ends.

A practical handoff checklist for property teams coordinating copy, assets and updates.

01 · Prepare an approved source

Keep property facts, approved descriptions and authorized photos together. Note the source and owner of each fact. Have the responsible property professional verify all public claims.

02 · Track each destination

List where the listing should appear, the approved version and the publication owner. Mark required changes separately from optional enhancements so revisions do not drift between channels.

03 · Plan for changes

Decide who updates availability, price and contact details. Retain the published URLs so the team can check every destination when something changes. A workflow review does not replace professional advertising review.
