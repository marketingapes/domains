All articles

13 plain-English guides to how money moves through a small business. New to the topic? Start with what working capital is, then work through the cash cycle. Every guide uses illustrative round numbers and shows the arithmetic, so you can swap in your own.

The guides are grouped into four themes. Basics covers the formula and the ratios lenders look at. The cash cycle covers receivables, inventory and supplier terms, the day-to-day levers that decide how long your money is tied up. Planning covers seasonal swings, forecasting, growth and selling a business. Financing compares the ways businesses fund gaps and how to work out what each one really costs.
