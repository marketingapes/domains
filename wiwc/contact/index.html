Good reasons to write

  • Corrections. If you think a figure, formula or explanation is wrong or out of date, tell us which article and what you found. We take accuracy seriously and update pages when we get something wrong.
  • Article suggestions. A working capital or cash flow question you could not find a clear answer to? It may become our next guide.
  • Privacy requests. Questions about cookies, analytics or data, as described in our privacy policy.
  • Advertising and partnership enquiries. We keep editorial content independent, so we do not accept paid articles or paid links.

What we cannot help with

We are an educational publisher, not a lender, broker or accounting practice. We cannot review your accounts, recommend a specific lender, arrange financing, or give personal financial, tax or legal advice. For those, please speak to a qualified accountant, financial adviser or lawyer where you are based. Please do not send us bank statements, account numbers or other sensitive financial information.
