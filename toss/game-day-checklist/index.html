A useful starting point

Make the next game day easier to organize.

A lightweight checklist for the people handling logistics around the game.

01 · Confirm the essentials

Check the organizer’s current time, location, arrival instructions and equipment rules. Keep one approved source of truth for changes so people do not act on an old message.

02 · Assign the handoffs

Name who handles equipment, transport coordination and updates. Share only the contact details the group needs, and use the organizer’s approved channel for participant information.

03 · Leave a useful note

After the event, record missing items and what to change next time. Reuse the checklist, but recheck time-sensitive details before every event.
