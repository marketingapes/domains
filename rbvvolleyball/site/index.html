Athlete of the Week

September 14–19

Avery Dayus

Senior • #6 • Middle Blocker / Opposite • Captain

Avery was a clear leader all week, making an impact with blocks and kills. At this weekend’s tournament, she led the team in kills, finished among the top three in aces, and was one of our dig leaders. Her all-around play and leadership showed what it means to be a senior captain. Congratulations, Avery — keep leading the way!

September 7–11

Summer Raver

Junior • #4 • Outside Hitter • Captain

Summer continues to grow as an outside hitter while leading with a strong work ethic, a kind heart, and consistent effort. She leads by example on and off the court and works to improve every practice and match. Keep it up, Summer!
