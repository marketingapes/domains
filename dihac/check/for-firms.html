The cases you take find you.

Claim Check is a free public tool. People describe what happened in plain words and instantly see what kind of claim it sounds like, what type of firm handles it, and whether firms are taking cases like theirs in their state.

1

They describe it. "I was rear-ended." "I took Ozempic." "I was abused in juvenile hall." No forms, no legal terms.

2

It sorts them. Car, truck, rideshare, premises, dog bite, abuse, pharmaceutical, device, toxic exposure, med mal, work injury, employment — and non-injury matters.

3

Your case types light up green. When the claim type and state match what your firm takes, the person is told firms are actively looking for their case.

What your firm gets

  • Pre-sorted people. Every visitor is tagged by claim type and state before anyone talks to them.
  • Only your case types. You tell us what you take and where. Everything else is pointed elsewhere, politely.
  • Your turn-down list, respected. What you don't take never shows as "accepting."
