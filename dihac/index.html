What happens

Three steps. One dynamic path.

  1. 01

    You describe it

    Plain words. No forms, no legal terms, no account.

  2. 02

    We ask what matters

    A few follow-ups based on what you described — when, where, who else, what you already have.

  3. 03

    The next path changes

    Vehicle-related matters continue to Nearest Injury Lawyers. Other recognised matters continue to Best Tort Lawyers. If key information is missing, you already have counsel, or we cannot tell what kind of matter this is, we hold it for a person instead of guessing.
